# CONSERVE 2021 Checklist

> Scope: The CONSERVE 2021 statement provides guidelines for reporting trial protocols and completed trials that have been modified due to the COVID-19 pandemic and other extenuating circumstances. It serves as an extension to both CONSORT and SPIRIT statements.
>
> Reference: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2781349

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- CONSERVE extends CONSORT and SPIRIT - use this in addition to the primary checklist.

## Section 1: Extenuating Circumstances

- [ ] **1a. Identification:** Identify and describe the extenuating circumstances that necessitated trial modifications.
- [ ] **1b. Timeline:** Specify when the extenuating circumstances began and, if applicable, when they ended.
- [ ] **1c. Geographic scope:** Describe the geographic areas affected by the extenuating circumstances.
- [ ] **1d. Impact assessment:** Assess and describe how the extenuating circumstances affected or were likely to affect the trial.

## Section 2: Important Modifications

- [ ] **2a. Modification identification:** Identify and describe all important modifications made to the trial due to extenuating circumstances.
- [ ] **2b. Rationale:** Provide the rationale for each important modification.
- [ ] **2c. Implementation timeline:** Specify when each modification was implemented.
- [ ] **2d. Participant notification:** Describe how participants were informed of modifications and whether additional consent was obtained.
- [ ] **2e. Protocol amendments:** Reference any formal protocol amendments related to the modifications.
- [ ] **2f. Regulatory approval:** Describe regulatory approvals obtained for modifications, if applicable.

## Section 3: Responsible Parties

- [ ] **3a. Decision-making authority:** Identify who had authority to make decisions about trial modifications.
- [ ] **3b. Communication channels:** Describe communication channels used for decision-making and implementation.
- [ ] **3c. Oversight bodies:** Identify oversight bodies (e.g., data monitoring committees, ethics committees) involved in approving modifications.
- [ ] **3d. Stakeholder consultation:** Describe consultation with relevant stakeholders (participants, investigators, sponsors, regulators).

## Section 4: Interim Data Analyses

- [ ] **4a. Unplanned analyses:** Report any unplanned interim analyses conducted due to extenuating circumstances.
- [ ] **4b. Modified analyses:** Describe modifications to planned interim analyses.
- [ ] **4c. Decision criteria:** Specify decision criteria used for interim analyses related to extenuating circumstances.
- [ ] **4d. Statistical considerations:** Address statistical implications of modifications and interim analyses.
- [ ] **4e. Bias assessment:** Assess potential for bias introduced by modifications and interim analyses.

## Additional Considerations

- [ ] **5a. Participant safety:** Describe measures taken to ensure participant safety during extenuating circumstances.
- [ ] **5b. Data integrity:** Address how data integrity was maintained despite modifications.
- [ ] **5c. Generalizability:** Discuss implications of modifications for generalizability of results.
- [ ] **5d. Lessons learned:** Provide insights that may inform future trials in similar circumstances.
