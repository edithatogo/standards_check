# CONSORT for Equity

> Scope: The CONSORT-Equity 2017 extension provides guidance for better reporting of health equity in randomized trials, helping to ensure transparent reporting of equity-relevant aspects of trial design, conduct, and results.
>
> Reference: Welch VA, Norheim OF, Jull J, Cookson R, Sommerfelt H, Tugwell P; CONSORT-Equity and Boston Equity Symposium. CONSORT-Equity 2017 extension and elaboration for better reporting of health equity in randomised trials. BMJ. 2017;359:j5085.

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the CONSORT-Equity extension checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification as a health equity relevant randomized trial:** Indicate in the title or abstract that the trial is health equity relevant, if applicable.

## Abstract

- [ ] **2. Structured summary:** Present structured summary including, as applicable: background; objectives; methods (including study setting, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size, randomization, blinding, and statistical methods); results (including participant flow, recruitment, baseline data, numbers analyzed, outcomes, and adverse events); and conclusions. Include information on health equity relevance.

## Introduction

- [ ] **3. Background/rationale:** Explain the scientific background and rationale for the trial, including the health equity relevance and justification for the study.

- [ ] **4. Objectives:** Clearly state the specific objectives or hypotheses, including equity-related objectives, and any prespecified subgroup or interaction analyses related to equity.

## Methods

- [ ] **5. Trial design:** Describe the trial design (such as parallel, crossover, or factorial) and methods used to generate the randomization sequence; specify whether the trial addresses health equity.

- [ ] **6. Participants:** Eligibility criteria for participants and the settings and locations where the data were collected; describe whether equity considerations were part of the eligibility criteria.

- [ ] **7. Interventions:** Clearly describe the interventions for each group and how they were delivered, including any adaptations made for different equity groups.

- [ ] **8. Outcomes:** Clearly define the primary and secondary outcome measures, including how and when they were assessed; specify whether outcomes are equity relevant.

- [ ] **9. Sample size:** Explain how the sample size was determined and, if applicable, whether it was based on an equity-related outcome or subgroup analysis.

- [ ] **10. Randomization:** Describe the mechanism used to generate the randomization sequence, including any stratification or blocking (including by equity-related factors).

- [ ] **11. Blinding:** Describe who was blinded after assignment to interventions (for example, participants, personnel, outcome assessors) and how.

- [ ] **12. Statistical methods:** Describe the statistical methods used to compare groups for primary and secondary outcomes, including any methods for adjusting for confounding or clustering; specify methods for equity-related analyses.

## Results

- [ ] **13. Participant flow:** Describe the flow of participants, including those who were excluded from the analysis, and reasons for drop-outs and exclusions; include equity group information where applicable.

- [ ] **14. Recruitment:** Report dates defining the periods of recruitment and follow-up.

- [ ] **15. Baseline data:** Give characteristics of participants at baseline and provide information on equity-relevant characteristics (such as socioeconomic status, race/ethnicity, sex, age groups, education, geographic location, etc.).

- [ ] **16. Numbers analyzed:** Report the numbers of participants in each group analyzed for primary and secondary outcomes, indicating whether analysis was by original assigned groups; include equity subgroup analyses.

- [ ] **17. Outcomes and estimation:** Report results for each outcome and each group, with confidence intervals and precise p-values; report equity-related outcomes and subgroup analyses.

- [ ] **18. Ancillary analyses:** Report results of any other analyses performed, including subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses, especially those related to equity.

## Discussion

- [ ] **19. Limitations:** Discuss limitations of the trial, including potential sources of bias and imprecision, and limitations specific to equity-related analyses.

- [ ] **20. Generalizability:** Comment on the generalizability (external validity) of the trial results, particularly to equity-relevant populations.

- [ ] [**21. Interpretation:** Discuss the results, taking into account the study's limitations and the results from other relevant studies, particularly those with equity considerations.

## Other Information

- [ ] **22. Registration:** Provide the trial registration number and name of the registry.

- [ ] **23. Protocol:** Indicate where the full trial protocol can be accessed.

- [ ] **24. Funding:** Describe sources of funding and other support (such as supply of drugs), and the role of funders.
