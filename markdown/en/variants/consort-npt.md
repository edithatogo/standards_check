# CONSORT for Non-pharmacological Treatment Trials

> Scope: The CONSORT extension for Non-pharmacological Treatment Trials (NPTs) provides reporting guidelines for trials evaluating interventions that do not involve drugs or devices. This extension addresses specific methodological considerations of NPTs, including intervention complexity, provider expertise, and implementation fidelity.
>
> Reference: Boutron I, Altman DG, Moher D, Schulz KF, Ravaud P; CONSORT NPT Group. CONSORT Statement for Randomized Trials of Nonpharmacologic Treatments: A 2017 Update and a CONSORT Extension for Nonpharmacologic Trial Abstracts. Ann Intern Med. 2017;167(1):40-47.

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the CONSORT NPT extension checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification as a non-pharmacological treatment trial:** Indicate in the title that the trial evaluates a non-pharmacological treatment and specify the type of treatment.

## Abstract

- [ ] **2a. Structured summary:** Present a structured summary including, as applicable: background; objectives; methods (including study setting, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size, randomization, blinding, and statistical methods); results (including participant flow, recruitment, baseline data, numbers analyzed, outcomes, and adverse events); and conclusions.

- [ ] **2b. Trial design in abstract:** Clearly state in the abstract that the trial evaluates a non-pharmacological treatment.

## Introduction

- [ ] **3a. Scientific background:** Explain the scientific background and rationale for the trial, including justification for using a non-pharmacological treatment.

- [ ] **3b. Specific objectives:** Clearly state the specific objectives or hypotheses, including any prespecified subgroup analyses related to the non-pharmacological treatment.

## Methods

- [ ] **4a. Trial design description:** Describe the trial design and methods used to generate the randomization sequence.

- [ ] **4b. Important changes:** Describe any important changes to methods after trial commencement (such as eligibility criteria), with reasons.

- [ ] **5a. Eligibility criteria:** Specify eligibility criteria for participants, including any restrictions related to the non-pharmacological treatment.

- [ ] **5b. Settings and locations:** Describe the settings and locations where the data were collected, including any variations in implementation across sites.

- [ ] **6a. Interventions: overview:** Clearly describe the non-pharmacological treatments for each group and how they were delivered, including the theoretical framework or rationale for the treatment.

- [ ] **6b. Interventions: providers:** Specify who provided the non-pharmacological treatments, their expertise, and any training they received.

- [ ] **6c. Interventions: tailoring:** Describe whether and how the non-pharmacological treatments were tailored to individual participants or sites.

- [ ] **6d. Interventions: adherence:** Describe how adherence to the non-pharmacological treatments was assessed and maximized.

- [ ] **6e. Interventions: complexity:** Describe the complexity of the non-pharmacological treatments, including the number and types of components, their interdependencies, and the flexibility in delivery.

- [ ] **7a. Outcomes: overview:** Provide an overview of all outcomes measured in the trial, including primary, secondary, and other prespecified outcomes.

- [ ] **7b. Outcomes: definitions:** For each outcome, provide a precise definition including the measurement variable (what is being measured), method of measurement (how it is measured), metric (how it is summarized), and time point(s) of measurement.

- [ ] **7c. Outcomes: changes:** If any outcomes were modified after the trial commenced, describe the changes and explain why they were made.

- [ ] **8a. Sample size determination:** Explain how the sample size was determined, including assumptions about the effect size of the non-pharmacological treatment.

- [ ] **8b. Interim analyses:** If applicable, explain any interim analyses and stopping guidelines.

- [ ] **9a. Randomization sequence:** Describe the mechanism used to generate the randomization sequence, including any stratification or blocking.

- [ ] **9b. Allocation concealment:** Describe the mechanism used to implement the random allocation sequence (such as central telephone; web-based), describing any steps taken to conceal the sequence until treatments were assigned.

- [ ] **10. Blinding:** Describe who was blinded after assignment to treatments (for example, participants, personnel, outcome assessors) and how; explain any challenges specific to blinding non-pharmacological treatments.

- [ ] **11a. Statistical methods: overview:** Describe the statistical methods used to compare groups for primary and secondary outcomes, including any methods for adjusting for confounding.

- [ ] **11b. Statistical methods: NPT specific:** Specify methods for handling missing data, multiplicity adjustments, and any methods specific to analyzing non-pharmacological treatments.

## Results

- [ ] **12. Participant flow:** Describe the flow of participants, including those who were excluded from the analysis, and reasons for drop-outs and exclusions.

- [ ] **13a. Recruitment periods:** Report dates defining the periods of recruitment and follow-up.

- [ ] **13b. Reasons for trial end:** Report reasons why the trial ended or was stopped.

- [ ] **14. Baseline data:** Give characteristics of participants at baseline, including factors that might affect the outcomes.

- [ ] **15. Numbers analyzed:** Report the numbers of participants in each group analyzed for each outcome, indicating whether analysis was by original assigned groups.

- [ ] **16a. Outcomes and estimation:** Report results for each outcome and each group, with confidence intervals and precise p-values.

- [ ] **16b. Outcomes: NPT specific:** Report assessments of treatment fidelity, provider adherence, and any modifications made during the trial.

- [ ] **17. Ancillary analyses:** Report results of any other analyses performed, including subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses.

- [ ] **18. Harms:** Report all important harms or unintended effects in each group.

## Discussion

- [ ] **19. Limitations:** Discuss limitations of the trial, including potential sources of bias and imprecision, and limitations specific to non-pharmacological treatments.

- [ ] **20. Generalizability:** Comment on the generalizability (external validity) of the trial results, particularly to other settings where non-pharmacological treatments might be used.

- [ ] **21. Interpretation:** Discuss the results, taking into account the study's limitations and the results from other relevant studies; interpret the clinical significance of the non-pharmacological treatment.

## Other Information

- [ ] **22. Registration:** Provide the trial registration number and name of the registry; indicate if the non-pharmacological treatment was specified in the registry.

- [ ] **23. Protocol:** Indicate where the full trial protocol can be accessed.

- [ ] **24. Funding:** Describe sources of funding and other support, and the role of funders.
