# PRISMA-Equity Checklist

> Scope: The PRISMA-Equity 2012 Extension provides guidance for reporting equity-focused systematic reviews in order to help reviewers identify, extract, and synthesize evidence on health equity.
>
> Reference: Welch V, Petticrew M, Tugwell P, Moher D, O'Neill J, Waters E, White H; PRISMA-Equity Bellagio group. PRISMA-Equity 2012 Extension: Reporting Guidelines for Systematic Reviews with a Focus on Health Equity. PLoS Med. 2012;9(10):e1001333.

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the PRISMA-Equity extension checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification:** Identify equity as a focus of the review, if relevant, using the term equity.

## Abstract

- [ ] **2. Structured summary:** State research question(s) related to health equity.
- [ ] **2A. Results:** Present results of health equity analyses (e.g., subgroup analyses or meta-regression).
- [ ] **2B. Applicability:** Describe extent and limits of applicability to disadvantaged populations of interest.

## Introduction

- [ ] **3. Rationale:** Describe assumptions about mechanism(s) by which the intervention is assumed to have an impact on health equity.
- [ ] **3A. Logic model:** Provide the logic model/analytical framework, if done, to show the pathways through which the intervention is assumed to affect health equity and how it was developed.
- [ ] **4. Objectives:** Describe how disadvantage was defined if used as criterion in the review (e.g., for selecting studies, conducting analyses, or judging applicability).
- [ ] **4A. Research questions:** State the research questions being addressed with reference to health equity.

## Methods

- [ ] **6. Eligibility criteria:** Describe the rationale for including particular study designs related to equity research questions.
- [ ] **6A. Outcomes:** Describe the rationale for including the outcomes (e.g., how these are relevant to reducing inequity).
- [ ] **7. Information sources:** Describe information sources (e.g., health, non-health, and grey literature sources) that were searched that are of specific relevance to address the equity questions of the review.
- [ ] **8. Search strategy:** Describe the broad search strategy and terms used to address equity questions of the review.
- [ ] **11. Data items:** List and define data items related to equity, where such data were sought (e.g., using PROGRESS-Plus or other criteria, context).
- [ ] **14. Synthesis of results:** Describe methods of synthesizing findings on health inequities (e.g., presenting both relative and absolute differences between groups).
- [ ] **16. Additional analyses:** Describe methods of additional synthesis approaches related to equity questions, if done, indicating which were pre-specified.

## Results

- [ ] **18. Study characteristics:** Present the population characteristics that relate to the equity questions across the relevant PROGRESS-Plus or other factors of interest.
- [ ] **21. Synthesis of results:** Present the results of synthesizing findings on inequities (see item 14).
- [ ] **23. Additional analysis:** Give the results of additional synthesis approaches related to equity objectives, if done, (see item 16).

## Discussion

- [ ] **26. Conclusions:** Present extent and limits of applicability to disadvantaged populations of interest and describe the evidence and logic underlying those judgments.
- [ ] **26A. Implications:** Provide implications for research, practice, or policy related to equity where relevant (e.g., types of research needed to address unanswered questions).
