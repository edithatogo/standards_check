# CONSORT for Stepped-wedge Cluster Trials

> Scope: The CONSORT extension for Stepped-wedge Cluster Trials (SW-CRTs) provides reporting guidelines for trials using a stepped-wedge cluster design where clusters are randomly assigned to sequences that dictate the time periods during which they receive the intervention. This extension addresses specific methodological considerations of SW-CRTs, including timing of crossover, carryover effects, and within-cluster correlation.
>
> Reference: Hemming K, Taljaard M, McKenzie JE, Hooper R, Copas A, Thompson JA, Dixon-Woods M, Aldcroft A, Doussau A, Grayling M, Kristunas C, Goldstein CE, Campbell MK, Girling A, Eldridge S, Campbell MJ, Lilford RJ, Weijer C, Forbes AB, Grimshaw JM. Reporting of stepped wedge cluster randomised trials: extension of the CONSORT 2010 statement with explanation and elaboration. BMJ. 2018;363:k1614.

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the CONSORT SW-CRT extension checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification as a stepped-wedge cluster trial:** Indicate in the title that the trial is a stepped-wedge cluster randomised trial and specify the intervention being evaluated.

## Abstract

- [ ] **2a. Structured summary:** Present a structured summary including, as applicable: background; objectives; methods (including study setting, clusters, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size, randomization, blinding, and statistical methods); results (including participant flow, recruitment, baseline data, numbers analyzed, outcomes, and adverse events); and conclusions.

- [ ] **2b. Trial design in abstract:** Clearly state in the abstract that the trial is a stepped-wedge cluster randomised trial.

## Introduction

- [ ] **3a. Scientific background:** Explain the scientific background and rationale for the trial, including justification for using a stepped-wedge cluster design.

- [ ] **3b. Specific objectives:** Clearly state the specific objectives or hypotheses, including any prespecified subgroup analyses related to the stepped-wedge design.

## Methods

- [ ] **4a. Trial design description:** Describe the stepped-wedge cluster trial design, including the number of sequences, steps, and time periods, and methods used to generate the randomization sequence.

- [ ] **4b. Important changes:** Describe any important changes to methods after trial commencement (such as eligibility criteria), with reasons.

- [ ] **5a. Eligibility criteria for clusters:** Specify eligibility criteria for clusters and participants, including any restrictions on cluster size or composition.

- [ ] **5b. Settings and locations:** Describe the settings and locations where the data were collected, including any variations across steps.

- [ ] **6. Interventions:** Clearly describe the interventions for each group and how they were delivered, including details of the stepped implementation across clusters.

- [ ] **7a. Outcomes: overview:** Provide an overview of all outcomes measured in the trial, including primary, secondary, and other prespecified outcomes.

- [ ] **7b. Outcomes: definitions:** For each outcome, provide a precise definition including the measurement variable (what is being measured), method of measurement (how it is measured), metric (how it is summarized), and time point(s) of measurement.

- [ ] **7c. Outcomes: changes:** If any outcomes were modified after the trial commenced, describe the changes and explain why they were made.

- [ ] **8a. Sample size determination:** Explain how the sample size was determined, including assumptions about intracluster correlation, within-cluster autocorrelation, and the number of steps and sequences.

- [ ] **8b. Interim analyses:** If applicable, explain any interim analyses and stopping guidelines, including considerations specific to the stepped-wedge design.

- [ ] **9a. Randomization sequence:** Describe the mechanism used to generate the randomization sequence, including the method for assigning clusters to sequences.

- [ ] **9b. Allocation concealment:** Describe the mechanism used to implement the random allocation sequence, describing any steps taken to conceal the sequence until interventions were assigned.

- [ ] **10. Blinding:** Describe who was blinded after assignment to interventions (for example, participants, personnel, outcome assessors) and how; explain how blinding was maintained across steps.

- [ ] **11a. Statistical methods: overview:** Describe the statistical methods used to compare groups for primary and secondary outcomes, including any methods for adjusting for confounding or clustering.

- [ ] **11b. Statistical methods: SW-CRT specific:** Specify methods for handling carryover effects, time effects, and within-cluster correlation; describe approaches for dealing with missing data and multiplicity adjustments.

## Results

- [ ] **12. Participant flow:** Describe the flow of clusters and participants, including those who were excluded from the analysis, and reasons for drop-outs and exclusions; provide flow diagrams showing the stepped implementation across clusters.

- [ ] **13a. Recruitment periods:** Report dates defining the periods of recruitment and follow-up for each step.

- [ ] **13b. Reasons for trial end:** Report reasons why the trial ended or was stopped.

- [ ] **14. Baseline data:** Give characteristics of clusters and participants at baseline for each step, including factors that might affect the outcomes.

- [ ] **15. Numbers analyzed:** Report the numbers of clusters and participants in each group analyzed for each outcome, indicating whether analysis was by original assigned groups; report numbers for each step.

- [ ] **16a. Outcomes and estimation:** Report results for each outcome and each group, with confidence intervals and precise p-values; report results for each step.

- [ ] **16b. Outcomes: SW-CRT specific:** Report assessments of carryover effects, time effects, and within-cluster correlation; present results adjusted for these factors as appropriate.

- [ ] **17. Ancillary analyses:** Report results of any other analyses performed, including subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses, especially those related to the stepped-wedge design.

- [ ] **18. Harms:** Report all important harms or unintended effects in each group.

## Discussion

- [ ] **19. Limitations:** Discuss limitations of the trial, including potential sources of bias and imprecision, and limitations specific to the stepped-wedge cluster design.

- [ ] **20. Generalizability:** Comment on the generalizability (external validity) of the trial results, particularly to other settings where stepped-wedge cluster trials might be used.

- [ ] **21. Interpretation:** Discuss the results, taking into account the study's limitations and the results from other relevant studies, particularly those with stepped-wedge designs.

## Other Information

- [ ] **22. Registration:** Provide the trial registration number and name of the registry; indicate if the stepped-wedge design was specified in the registry.

- [ ] **23. Protocol:** Indicate where the full trial protocol can be accessed, including details of the stepped-wedge design and analysis plan.

- [ ] **24. Funding:** Describe sources of funding and other support, and the role of funders.
