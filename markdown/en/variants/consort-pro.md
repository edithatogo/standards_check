# CONSORT for Patient-reported Outcomes

> Scope: The CONSORT extension for Patient-reported Outcomes (PROs) provides reporting guidelines for trials where PROs are primary or important secondary endpoints. This extension addresses specific methodological considerations of PROs, including instrument selection, administration, and interpretation.
>
> Reference: Calvert M, Blazeby J, Altman DG, Revicki DA, Moher D, Brundage MD; CONSORT PRO Group. Reporting of patient-reported outcomes in randomized trials: the CONSORT PRO extension. JAMA. 2013;309(8):814-22.

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the CONSORT PRO extension checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification as a PRO trial:** Indicate in the title that the trial includes patient-reported outcomes and specify the primary PRO instrument(s) used.

## Abstract

- [ ] **2a. Structured summary:** Present a structured summary including, as applicable: background; objectives; methods (including study setting, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size, randomization, blinding, and statistical methods); results (including participant flow, recruitment, baseline data, numbers analyzed, outcomes, and adverse events); and conclusions.

- [ ] **2b. PRO results in abstract:** Report the primary PRO results in the abstract, including the effect size and confidence intervals.

## Introduction

- [ ] **3a. Scientific background:** Explain the scientific background and rationale for the trial, including the importance of patient-reported outcomes in the condition being studied.

- [ ] **3b. Specific objectives:** Clearly state the specific objectives or hypotheses related to patient-reported outcomes, including the primary and secondary PRO endpoints.

## Methods

- [ ] **4a. Trial design description:** Describe the trial design and methods used to generate the randomization sequence.

- [ ] **4b. Important changes:** Describe any important changes to methods after trial commencement (such as eligibility criteria), with reasons.

- [ ] **5a. Eligibility criteria:** Specify eligibility criteria for participants, including any restrictions related to the ability to complete PRO assessments.

- [ ] **5b. Settings and locations:** Describe the settings and locations where the data were collected.

- [ ] **6. Interventions:** Clearly describe the interventions for each group and how they were delivered.

- [ ] **7a. PRO outcomes: overview:** Provide an overview of all patient-reported outcomes measured in the trial, including primary, secondary, and other prespecified PRO endpoints.

- [ ] **7b. PRO outcomes: instruments:** For each PRO instrument, provide details of the instrument used including name, version, domain(s) measured, recall period, response options, and scoring method.

- [ ] **7c. PRO outcomes: validity and reliability:** Provide evidence of PRO instrument validity and reliability in the study population, including the person completing the PRO and methods of administration.

- [ ] **7d. PRO outcomes: changes:** If any PRO outcomes were modified after the trial commenced, describe the changes and explain why they were made.

- [ ] **8a. Sample size determination:** Explain how the sample size was determined, including assumptions about the PRO effect size and any adjustments for missing PRO data.

- [ ] **8b. Interim analyses:** If applicable, explain any interim analyses and stopping guidelines.

- [ ] **9a. Randomization sequence:** Describe the mechanism used to generate the randomization sequence, including any stratification or blocking.

- [ ] **9b. Allocation concealment:** Describe the mechanism used to implement the random allocation sequence (such as central telephone; web-based), describing any steps taken to conceal the sequence until interventions were assigned.

- [ ] **10. Blinding:** Describe who was blinded after assignment to interventions (for example, participants, personnel, outcome assessors) and how.

- [ ] **11a. Statistical methods: overview:** Describe the statistical methods used to compare groups for primary and secondary outcomes, including any methods for adjusting for confounding.

- [ ] **11b. Statistical methods: PRO specific:** Specify methods for handling missing PRO data, approaches to PRO data analysis (e.g., summary scores, individual items), methods for interpreting PRO results (e.g., minimally important differences), and any multiplicity adjustments.

## Results

- [ ] **12. Participant flow:** Describe the flow of participants, including those who were excluded from the analysis, and reasons for drop-outs and exclusions; report the number and reasons for missing PRO data.

- [ ] **13a. Recruitment periods:** Report dates defining the periods of recruitment and follow-up.

- [ ] **13b. Reasons for trial end:** Report reasons why the trial ended or was stopped.

- [ ] **14. Baseline data:** Give characteristics of participants at baseline, including factors that might affect PRO responses.

- [ ] **15. Numbers analyzed:** Report the numbers of participants in each group analyzed for each PRO outcome, indicating whether analysis was by original assigned groups; report the number of participants with evaluable PRO data.

- [ ] **16a. PRO outcomes and estimation:** Report PRO results for each group, with confidence intervals and precise p-values; report effect sizes for PRO endpoints.

- [ ] **16b. PRO outcomes: interpretation:** Report PRO results in terms of clinical interpretability (e.g., minimally important differences, responder analyses) where appropriate.

- [ ] **17. Ancillary analyses:** Report results of any other PRO analyses performed, including subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses.

- [ ] **18. Harms:** Report all important harms or unintended effects in each group, including those captured by PRO instruments.

## Discussion

- [ ] **19. Limitations:** Discuss limitations of the trial, including potential sources of bias and imprecision, and limitations specific to PRO assessment.

- [ ] **20. Generalizability:** Comment on the generalizability (external validity) of the trial results, particularly regarding PRO findings.

- [ ] **21. Interpretation:** Discuss the PRO results, taking into account the study's limitations and the results from other relevant studies; interpret the clinical significance of PRO findings.

## Other Information

- [ ] **22. Registration:** Provide the trial registration number and name of the registry; indicate if PRO endpoints were specified in the registry.

- [ ] **23. Protocol:** Indicate where the full trial protocol can be accessed, including details of PRO endpoints and analysis plans.

- [ ] **24. Funding:** Describe sources of funding and other support, and the role of funders.
