# CONSORT Extension for Pilot and Feasibility Trials

> Scope: The CONSORT Extension for Pilot and Feasibility Trials provides reporting guidelines for randomised pilot and feasibility trials conducted in advance of a future definitive RCT. This extension modifies several CONSORT 2010 items and adds pilot-specific reporting requirements.
>
> Reference: https://doi.org/10.1186/s40814-016-0105-8

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- This extension modifies 8 CONSORT 2010 items and adds 5 new items specific to pilot trials.
- Report progression criteria and feasibility objectives clearly.

## Title and Abstract

- [ ] **1a. Identification:** Identification as a pilot or feasibility randomised trial in the title
- [ ] **1b. Structured summary:** Structured summary including objectives, trial design, randomisation, blinding, participant numbers analysed, recruitment/follow-up rates, planned progression criteria, main findings including feasibility/progression criteria, and conclusions including implications for progression to definitive trial

## Introduction

- [ ] **2a. Background and rationale:** Scientific background and explanation of rationale for future definitive trial, and reasons for randomised pilot trial
- [ ] **2b. Objectives:** Specific objectives and research questions for pilot trial. Additionally, objectives and hypotheses of future definitive RCT

## Methods

### Trial design

- [ ] **3a. Description:** Description of pilot trial design including type of pilot trial (external/internal), methods used to protect against sources of bias, and description of future definitive RCT design
- [ ] **3b. Changes:** Important changes to methods after pilot trial commencement (such as eligibility criteria), with reasons

### Participants

- [ ] **4a. Eligibility criteria:** Eligibility criteria for participants and settings where data were collected. Additionally, eligibility criteria for pilot trial and future definitive RCT
- [ ] **4b. Settings:** Settings and locations where the data were collected

### Interventions

- [ ] **5. Interventions:** The interventions for each group with sufficient details to allow replication, including how and when they were actually administered

### Outcomes

- [ ] **6a. Outcomes:** Completely defined pre-specified primary and secondary outcome measures for the future definitive RCT, including how and when they were assessed. Additionally, specify feasibility outcome measures for pilot trial
- [ ] **6b. Changes:** Any changes to trial outcomes after the pilot trial commenced, with reasons

### Sample size

- [ ] **7a. Sample size:** How sample size was determined for pilot trial. Additionally, if applicable, sample size calculation for future definitive RCT
- [ ] **7b. Interim analyses:** When applicable, explanation of any interim analyses and stopping guidelines

### Randomisation

- [ ] **8a. Sequence generation:** Method used to generate the random allocation sequence
- [ ] **8b. Allocation concealment:** Type of randomisation; details of any restriction (such as blocking and block size)
- [ ] **8c. Implementation:** Mechanism used to implement the random allocation sequence (such as sequentially numbered containers), describing any steps taken to conceal the sequence until interventions were assigned

### Blinding

- [ ] **9. Blinding:** If done, who was blinded after assignment to interventions (for example, participants, care providers, those assessing outcomes) and how

### Statistical methods

- [ ] **10. Statistical methods:** Statistical methods used to compare groups for primary and secondary outcomes; methods for additional analyses, such as subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses

### Progression criteria

- [ ] **11. Progression criteria:** Criteria used to judge whether to proceed with future definitive RCT and any amendments to these criteria

## Results

### Participant flow

- [ ] **12. Participant flow:** For each group, the numbers of participants randomly assigned, receiving intended treatment, and analysed for the primary outcome. Additionally, number screened and number eligible

### Recruitment

- [ ] **13a. Recruitment:** Dates defining the periods of recruitment and follow-up
- [ ] **13b. Stopped or ended:** Why the pilot trial ended or was stopped

### Baseline data

- [ ] **14. Baseline characteristics:** A table showing baseline demographic and clinical characteristics for each group

### Numbers analysed

- [ ] **15. Numbers analysed:** For each group, number of participants (denominator) included in each analysis and whether the analysis was by original assigned groups

### Outcomes and estimation

- [ ] **16. Outcomes:** For each primary and secondary outcome, results for each group, and the estimated effect size and its precision (such as 95% confidence interval). Additionally, for pilot trials, summary of feasibility outcome data and evidence for progression to definitive RCT
- [ ] **17. Ancillary analyses:** Results of any other analyses performed, including subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses, distinguishing pre-specified from exploratory

### Harms

- [ ] **18. Harms:** All important harms or unintended effects in each group

## Discussion

- [ ] **19. Limitations:** Trial limitations, addressing sources of potential bias, imprecision, and, if relevant, multiplicity of analyses
- [ ] **20. Generalisability:** Generalisability (external validity, applicability) of the pilot trial findings to the definitive trial, and other populations and settings
- [ ] **21. Interpretation:** Interpretation consistent with results, balancing benefits and harms, and considering other relevant evidence. Additionally, implications for progression from pilot to definitive trial

## Other information

- [ ] **22. Registration:** Registration number and name of trial registry
- [ ] **23. Protocol:** Where the pilot trial protocol can be accessed, if available
- [ ] **24. Funding:** Sources of funding and other support (such as supply of drugs), role of funders
