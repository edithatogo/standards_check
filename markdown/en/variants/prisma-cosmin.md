# PRISMA-COSMIN for Outcome Measurement Instruments (OMIs) 2024 Checklist

> Scope: The PRISMA-COSMIN for OMIs 2024 guideline provides reporting standards for systematic reviews of outcome measurement instruments. This checklist contains 54 (sub)items addressing title, abstract, plain language summary, open science, introduction, methods, results, discussion, and other information.
>
> Reference: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2024.111422

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the 54 (sub)items checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification:** Identify the report as a systematic review of outcome measurement instruments

## Abstract

- [ ] **2. Structured summary:** Provide a structured summary including background, objectives, eligibility criteria, information sources, risk of bias assessment methods, synthesis methods, included studies, results, limitations, interpretation, funding, and registration

## Plain Language Summary

- [ ] **3. Plain language summary:** Provide a plain language summary of the systematic review

## Open Science

- [ ] **4. Data sharing statement:** Provide a data sharing statement
- [ ] **5. Protocol registration:** Indicate whether the review protocol was registered and provide registration details
- [ ] **6. Availability of materials:** Indicate availability of materials (search strategy, data extraction forms, etc.)

## Introduction

- [ ] **7. Rationale:** Provide scientific background and rationale for the systematic review
- [ ] **8. Objectives:** Provide explicit statement of objectives and research questions

## Methods

### Eligibility Criteria

- [ ] **9. Population:** Specify eligibility criteria for populations
- [ ] **10. Measurement instruments:** Specify eligibility criteria for measurement instruments
- [ ] **11. Measurement properties:** Specify which measurement properties were evaluated
- [ ] **12. Context:** Specify contexts in which measurement instruments were evaluated

### Information Sources

- [ ] **13. Sources:** Specify all information sources used to identify studies
- [ ] **14. Search dates:** Specify dates when each source was last searched
- [ ] **15. Supplementary sources:** Specify supplementary sources used

### Search Strategy

- [ ] **16. Search strategy:** Present full search strategies for all sources
- [ ] **17. Selection process:** Specify process used to select studies

### Study Selection

- [ ] **18. Selection:** Specify number of records identified, screened, eligible, and included
- [ ] **19. Selection flow diagram:** Present flow diagram of study selection process

### Data Collection Process

- [ ] **20. Data collectors:** Specify who collected data
- [ ] **21. Data collection forms:** Describe data collection forms and procedures
- [ ] **22. Calibration:** Specify whether calibration exercises were performed

### Data Items

- [ ] **23. Variables collected:** List and define all variables collected
- [ ] **24. Instrument information:** Specify information collected about measurement instruments

### Study Risk of Bias Assessment

- [ ] **25. Risk of bias tool:** Specify tool used to assess risk of bias
- [ ] **26. Risk of bias assessors:** Specify who assessed risk of bias
- [ ] **27. Risk of bias calibration:** Specify whether calibration exercises were performed for risk of bias assessment

### Effect Measures

- [ ] **28. Effect measures:** Specify effect measures used for each measurement property

### Synthesis Methods

- [ ] **29. Synthesis methods:** Specify methods used to synthesize results
- [ ] **30. Heterogeneity assessment:** Specify methods used to assess statistical heterogeneity
- [ ] **31. Subgroup analyses:** Specify methods for subgroup analyses
- [ ] **32. Sensitivity analyses:** Specify methods for sensitivity analyses

### Meta-Bias

- [ ] **33. Reporting bias assessment:** Specify whether and how reporting bias was assessed

### Confidence in Cumulative Evidence

- [ ] **34. Certainty assessment:** Specify approach for rating certainty of evidence

## Results

### Study Selection

- [ ] **35. Study selection:** Present study selection results

### Study Characteristics

- [ ] **36. Study characteristics:** Present characteristics of included studies
- [ ] **37. Instrument characteristics:** Present characteristics of measurement instruments
- [ ] **38. Property characteristics:** Present characteristics of measurement properties

### Risk of Bias

- [ ] **39. Risk of bias presentation:** Present risk of bias assessment results

### Results of Individual Studies

- [ ] **40. Individual study results:** Present results of individual studies

### Synthesis of Results

- [ ] **41. Synthesis results:** Present synthesis results for each measurement property
- [ ] **42. Heterogeneity results:** Present heterogeneity assessment results
- [ ] **43. Subgroup results:** Present subgroup analysis results
- [ ] **44. Sensitivity results:** Present sensitivity analysis results

### Meta-Bias

- [ ] **45. Reporting bias results:** Present reporting bias assessment results

### Confidence in Cumulative Evidence

- [ ] **46. Certainty results:** Present certainty of evidence assessment results

## Discussion

- [ ] **47. Summary of evidence:** Summarize main findings with strength of evidence
- [ ] **48. Interpretation:** Discuss limitations and strength of evidence
- [ ] **49. Applicability:** Discuss applicability of findings in various contexts

## Other Information

- [ ] **50. Competing interests:** Disclose competing interests
- [ ] **51. Funding:** Specify funding sources and role of funders
- [ ] **52. Authors' contributions:** Specify role of authors
- [ ] **53. Acknowledgements:** Acknowledge contributions from others
- [ ] **54. Registration:** Provide registration details
