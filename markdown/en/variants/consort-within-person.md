# CONSORT for Within-person Trials

> Scope: The CONSORT extension for Within-person Trials provides reporting guidelines for trials where participants receive multiple interventions in a within-person design. This extension addresses specific methodological considerations of within-person trials, including carryover effects, period effects, and within-person correlation.
>
> Reference: Pandis N, Chung B, Scherer RW, Elbourne D, Altman DG. CONSORT 2010 statement: extension checklist for reporting within person randomised trials. BMJ. 2017;357:j2835.

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the CONSORT within-person extension checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification as a within-person trial:** Indicate in the title that the trial is a within-person randomised trial and specify the interventions being compared.

## Abstract

- [ ] **2a. Structured summary:** Present a structured summary including, as applicable: background; objectives; methods (including study setting, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size, randomization, blinding, and statistical methods); results (including participant flow, recruitment, baseline data, numbers analyzed, outcomes, and adverse events); and conclusions.

- [ ] **2b. Trial design in abstract:** Clearly state in the abstract that the trial is a within-person randomised trial.

## Introduction

- [ ] **3a. Scientific background:** Explain the scientific background and rationale for the trial, including justification for using a within-person design.

- [ ] **3b. Specific objectives:** Clearly state the specific objectives or hypotheses, including any prespecified subgroup analyses related to the within-person design.

## Methods

- [ ] **4a. Trial design description:** Describe the within-person trial design, including the number of periods and sequences, and methods used to generate the randomization sequence.

- [ ] **4b. Important changes:** Describe any important changes to methods after trial commencement (such as eligibility criteria), with reasons.

- [ ] **5a. Eligibility criteria:** Specify eligibility criteria for participants, including any restrictions related to the within-person design.

- [ ] **5b. Settings and locations:** Describe the settings and locations where the data were collected, including any variations across periods.

- [ ] **6. Interventions:** Clearly describe the interventions for each group and how they were delivered, including details of the within-person implementation.

- [ ] **7a. Outcomes: overview:** Provide an overview of all outcomes measured in the trial, including primary, secondary, and other prespecified outcomes.

- [ ] **7b. Outcomes: definitions:** For each outcome, provide a precise definition including the measurement variable (what is being measured), method of measurement (how it is measured), metric (how it is summarized), and time point(s) of measurement.

- [ ] **7c. Outcomes: changes:** If any outcomes were modified after the trial commenced, describe the changes and explain why they were made.

- [ ] **8a. Sample size determination:** Explain how the sample size was determined, including assumptions about within-person correlation and the number of periods.

- [ ] **8b. Interim analyses:** If applicable, explain any interim analyses and stopping guidelines, including considerations specific to the within-person design.

- [ ] **9a. Randomization sequence:** Describe the mechanism used to generate the randomization sequence, including the method for assigning interventions to periods.

- [ ] **9b. Allocation concealment:** Describe the mechanism used to implement the random allocation sequence, describing any steps taken to conceal the sequence until interventions were assigned.

- [ ] **10. Blinding:** Describe who was blinded after assignment to interventions (for example, participants, personnel, outcome assessors) and how; explain how blinding was maintained across periods.

- [ ] **11a. Statistical methods: overview:** Describe the statistical methods used to compare groups for primary and secondary outcomes, including any methods for adjusting for confounding.

- [ ] **11b. Statistical methods: within-person specific:** Specify methods for handling carryover effects, period effects, and within-person correlation; describe approaches for dealing with missing data and multiplicity adjustments.

## Results

- [ ] **12. Participant flow:** Describe the flow of participants, including those who were excluded from the analysis, and reasons for drop-outs and exclusions; provide flow diagrams showing the within-person implementation.

- [ ] **13a. Recruitment periods:** Report dates defining the periods of recruitment and follow-up for each period.

- [ ] **13b. Reasons for trial end:** Report reasons why the trial ended or was stopped.

- [ ] **14. Baseline data:** Give characteristics of participants at baseline for each period, including factors that might affect the outcomes.

- [ ] **15. Numbers analyzed:** Report the numbers of participants in each group analyzed for each outcome, indicating whether analysis was by original assigned groups; report numbers for each period.

- [ ] **16a. Outcomes and estimation:** Report results for each outcome and each group, with confidence intervals and precise p-values; report results for each period.

- [ ] **16b. Outcomes: within-person specific:** Report assessments of carryover effects, period effects, and within-person correlation; present results adjusted for these factors as appropriate.

- [ ] **17. Ancillary analyses:** Report results of any other analyses performed, including subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses, especially those related to the within-person design.

- [ ] **18. Harms:** Report all important harms or unintended effects in each group.

## Discussion

- [ ] **19. Limitations:** Discuss limitations of the trial, including potential sources of bias and imprecision, and limitations specific to the within-person design.

- [ ] **20. Generalizability:** Comment on the generalizability (external validity) of the trial results, particularly to other settings where within-person trials might be used.

- [ ] **21. Interpretation:** Discuss the results, taking into account the study's limitations and the results from other relevant studies, particularly those with within-person designs.

## Other Information

- [ ] **22. Registration:** Provide the trial registration number and name of the registry; indicate if the within-person design was specified in the registry.

- [ ] **23. Protocol:** Indicate where the full trial protocol can be accessed, including details of the within-person design and analysis plan.

- [ ] **24. Funding:** Describe sources of funding and other support, and the role of funders.
