# CONSORT for Outcomes

> Scope: The CONSORT-Outcomes 2022 extension provides evidence- and consensus-based guidance for reporting outcomes in published clinical trial reports, ensuring complete and transparent reporting of all outcomes measured in a trial.
>

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the CONSORT-Outcomes extension checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification:** Identify the report as a randomized trial.

## Abstract

- [ ] **2. Structured summary:** Present structured summary including, as applicable: background; objectives; methods (including study setting, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size, randomization, blinding, and statistical methods); results (including participant flow, recruitment, baseline data, numbers analyzed, outcomes, and adverse events); and conclusions. Include a comprehensive summary of all outcomes measured.

## Introduction

- [ ] **3. Background/rationale:** Explain the scientific background and rationale for the trial.

- [ ] **4. Objectives:** Clearly state the specific objectives or hypotheses, including all outcomes that will be reported.

## Methods

- [ ] **5. Trial design:** Describe the trial design (such as parallel, crossover, or factorial) and methods used to generate the randomization sequence.

- [ ] **6. Participants:** Eligibility criteria for participants and the settings and locations where the data were collected.

- [ ] **7. Interventions:** Clearly describe the interventions for each group and how they were delivered.

- [ ] **8a. Outcomes: overview:** Provide an overview of all outcomes measured in the trial, including primary, secondary, and other prespecified outcomes.

- [ ] **8a.1. Primary outcome(s):** For each primary outcome, provide a precise definition including the measurement variable (what is being measured), method of measurement (how it is measured), metric (how it is summarized), and time point(s) of measurement.

- [ ] **8a.2. Secondary outcome(s):** For each secondary outcome, provide a precise definition including the measurement variable (what is being measured), method of measurement (how it is measured), metric (how it is summarized), and time point(s) of measurement.

- [ ] **8a.3. Other prespecified outcomes:** For each other prespecified outcome, provide a precise definition including the measurement variable (what is being measured), method of measurement (how it is measured), metric (how it is summarized), and time point(s) of measurement.

- [ ] **8b. Outcomes: changes:** If any outcomes were modified after the trial commenced, describe the changes and explain why they were made.

- [ ] **9. Sample size:** Explain how the sample size was determined and, if applicable, whether it was based on any of the outcomes.

- [ ] **10. Randomization:** Describe the mechanism used to generate the randomization sequence, including any stratification or blocking.

- [ ] **11. Blinding:** Describe who was blinded after assignment to interventions (for example, participants, personnel, outcome assessors) and how.

- [ ] **12. Statistical methods:** Describe the statistical methods used to compare groups for primary and secondary outcomes, including any methods for adjusting for confounding or clustering; specify methods for handling missing data and multiplicity adjustments.

## Results

- [ ] **13. Participant flow:** Describe the flow of participants, including those who were excluded from the analysis, and reasons for drop-outs and exclusions.

- [ ] **14. Recruitment:** Report dates defining the periods of recruitment and follow-up.

- [ ] **15. Baseline data:** Give characteristics of participants at baseline.

- [ ] **16. Numbers analyzed:** Report the numbers of participants in each group analyzed for each outcome, indicating whether analysis was by original assigned groups.

- [ ] **17a. Outcomes and estimation: primary outcome(s):** For each primary outcome, report results for each group with confidence intervals and precise P values; include the effect estimate and its precision (such as mean difference and 95% CI or odds ratio and 95% CI).

- [ ] **17a.1. Primary outcome(s): completeness of reporting:** For each primary outcome, report whether all components of the outcome definition (measurement variable, method of measurement, metric, and time point of measurement) are reported.

- [ ] **17b. Outcomes and estimation: secondary outcome(s):** For each secondary outcome, report results for each group with confidence intervals and precise P values; include the effect estimate and its precision (such as mean difference and 95% CI or odds ratio and 95% CI).

- [ ] **17b.1. Secondary outcome(s): completeness of reporting:** For each secondary outcome, report whether all components of the outcome definition (measurement variable, method of measurement, metric, and time point of measurement) are reported.

- [ ] **17c. Outcomes and estimation: other prespecified outcomes:** For each other prespecified outcome, report results for each group with confidence intervals and precise P values; include the effect estimate and its precision (such as mean difference and 95% CI or odds ratio and 95% CI).

- [ ] **17c.1. Other prespecified outcomes: completeness of reporting:** For each other prespecified outcome, report whether all components of the outcome definition (measurement variable, method of measurement, metric, and time point of measurement) are reported.

- [ ] **18. Ancillary analyses:** Report results of any other analyses performed, including subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses.

## Discussion

- [ ] **19. Limitations:** Discuss limitations of the trial, including potential sources of bias and imprecision.

- [ ] **20. Generalizability:** Comment on the generalizability (external validity) of the trial results.

- [ ] **21. Interpretation:** Discuss the results, taking into account the study's limitations and the results from other relevant studies.

## Other Information

- [ ] **22. Registration:** Provide the trial registration number and name of the registry.

- [ ] **23. Protocol:** Indicate where the full trial protocol can be accessed.

- [ ] **24. Funding:** Describe sources of funding and other support (such as supply of drugs), and the role of funders.
