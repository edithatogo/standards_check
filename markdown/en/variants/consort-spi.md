# CONSORT for Social and Psychological Interventions

> Scope: The CONSORT extension for Social and Psychological Interventions (SPI) provides reporting guidelines for trials evaluating social and psychological interventions. This extension addresses specific methodological considerations of SPIs, including intervention complexity, theoretical frameworks, and implementation fidelity.
>
> Reference: Montgomery P, Grant S, Mayo-Wilson E, Macdonald G, Michie S, Hopewell S, Moher D; on behalf of the CONSORT-SPI Group. Reporting randomised trials of social and psychological interventions: the CONSORT-SPI 2018 Extension. Trials. 2018;19:407.

## Instructions
- Use task list items for checklist boxes; these become interactive checkboxes in PDF.
- Use a span with class `.textfield` for free‑text fields.
- Report all applicable items from the CONSORT-SPI extension checklist.

## Title

- [ ] **1. Identification as an SPI trial:** Indicate in the title that the trial evaluates a social or psychological intervention and specify the type of intervention.

## Abstract

- [ ] **2a. Structured summary:** Present a structured summary including, as applicable: background; objectives; methods (including study setting, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size, randomization, blinding, and statistical methods); results (including participant flow, recruitment, baseline data, numbers analyzed, outcomes, and adverse events); and conclusions.

- [ ] **2b. Trial design in abstract:** Clearly state in the abstract that the trial evaluates a social or psychological intervention.

## Introduction

- [ ] **3a. Scientific background:** Explain the scientific background and rationale for the trial, including the theoretical framework or model underlying the social or psychological intervention.

- [ ] **3b. Specific objectives:** Clearly state the specific objectives or hypotheses, including any prespecified subgroup analyses related to the social or psychological intervention.

## Methods

- [ ] **4a. Trial design description:** Describe the trial design and methods used to generate the randomization sequence.

- [ ] **4b. Important changes:** Describe any important changes to methods after trial commencement (such as eligibility criteria), with reasons.

- [ ] **5a. Eligibility criteria:** Specify eligibility criteria for participants, including any restrictions related to the social or psychological intervention.

- [ ] **5b. Settings and locations:** Describe the settings and locations where the data were collected, including any variations in implementation across sites.

- [ ] **6a. Interventions: overview:** Clearly describe the social or psychological interventions for each group and how they were delivered, including the theoretical framework or rationale for the intervention.

- [ ] **6b. Interventions: components:** Describe the components of the social or psychological interventions, their interdependencies, and the flexibility in delivery.

- [ ] **6c. Interventions: providers:** Specify who provided the social or psychological interventions, their expertise, and any training they received.

- [ ] **6d. Interventions: tailoring:** Describe whether and how the social or psychological interventions were tailored to individual participants or sites.

- [ ] **6e. Interventions: adherence:** Describe how adherence to the social or psychological interventions was assessed and maximized.

- [ ] **6f. Interventions: fidelity:** Describe how implementation fidelity of the social or psychological interventions was assessed and maximized.

- [ ] **7a. Outcomes: overview:** Provide an overview of all outcomes measured in the trial, including primary, secondary, and other prespecified outcomes.

- [ ] **7b. Outcomes: definitions:** For each outcome, provide a precise definition including the measurement variable (what is being measured), method of measurement (how it is measured), metric (how it is summarized), and time point(s) of measurement.

- [ ] **7c. Outcomes: changes:** If any outcomes were modified after the trial commenced, describe the changes and explain why they were made.

- [ ] **8a. Sample size determination:** Explain how the sample size was determined, including assumptions about the effect size of the social or psychological intervention.

- [ ] **8b. Interim analyses:** If applicable, explain any interim analyses and stopping guidelines.

- [ ] **9a. Randomization sequence:** Describe the mechanism used to generate the randomization sequence, including any stratification or blocking.

- [ ] **9b. Allocation concealment:** Describe the mechanism used to implement the random allocation sequence (such as central telephone; web-based), describing any steps taken to conceal the sequence until interventions were assigned.

- [ ] **10. Blinding:** Describe who was blinded after assignment to interventions (for example, participants, personnel, outcome assessors) and how; explain any challenges specific to blinding social or psychological interventions.

- [ ] **11a. Statistical methods: overview:** Describe the statistical methods used to compare groups for primary and secondary outcomes, including any methods for adjusting for confounding.

- [ ] **11b. Statistical methods: SPI specific:** Specify methods for handling missing data, multiplicity adjustments, and any methods specific to analyzing social or psychological interventions.

## Results

- [ ] **12. Participant flow:** Describe the flow of participants, including those who were excluded from the analysis, and reasons for drop-outs and exclusions.

- [ ] **13a. Recruitment periods:** Report dates defining the periods of recruitment and follow-up.

- [ ] **13b. Reasons for trial end:** Report reasons why the trial ended or was stopped.

- [ ] **14. Baseline data:** Give characteristics of participants at baseline, including factors that might affect the outcomes.

- [ ] **15. Numbers analyzed:** Report the numbers of participants in each group analyzed for each outcome, indicating whether analysis was by original assigned groups.

- [ ] **16a. Outcomes and estimation:** Report results for each outcome and each group, with confidence intervals and precise p-values.

- [ ] **16b. Outcomes: SPI specific:** Report assessments of intervention fidelity, provider adherence, and any modifications made during the trial.

- [ ] **17. Ancillary analyses:** Report results of any other analyses performed, including subgroup analyses and adjusted analyses.

- [ ] **18. Harms:** Report all important harms or unintended effects in each group.

## Discussion

- [ ] **19. Limitations:** Discuss limitations of the trial, including potential sources of bias and imprecision, and limitations specific to social or psychological interventions.

- [ ] **20. Generalizability:** Comment on the generalizability (external validity) of the trial results, particularly to other settings where social or psychological interventions might be used.

- [ ] **21. Interpretation:** Discuss the results, taking into account the study's limitations and the results from other relevant studies; interpret the clinical significance of the social or psychological intervention.

## Other Information

- [ ] **22. Registration:** Provide the trial registration number and name of the registry; indicate if the social or psychological intervention was specified in the registry.

- [ ] **23. Protocol:** Indicate where the full trial protocol can be accessed.

- [ ] **24. Funding:** Describe sources of funding and other support, and the role of funders.
