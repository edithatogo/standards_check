# Orthogonal methods: prospective qualification protocol 0.1.0

Part of the single integrated StandardFlow methods publication (#158), alongside E1-E14.
This is a planned supplement, not an independent paper, frozen design or result.
Existing protocols, experiment IDs and historical receipts remain unchanged.

## Comparative arms

On identical eligible, independently locked family-disjoint examples compare A:
deterministic extraction/rules/lexical baseline; B: qualified neural pipeline alone;
C: B plus calibrated selective prediction; D: B plus symbolic constraints;
E: B plus both; F: the complete provenance, format and release-gated pipeline.
Use paired ablations at fixed candidate and review budgets; record unavailable arms,
complete prediction, abstention, failure and counterexample denominators.
E15-E21 supplement, not replace, E2/E3/E5-E7/E9-E14.

## Sampling and inference

Define dependency components before assignment: co-publications, editions, extensions,
translations and shared obligations stay together. Development/model selection,
calibration-only and final evaluation components must be disjoint. Record calibration
as development in the current contract with a separately locked allocation manifest;
no calibration component can fit, select or tune the scored model. Before opening
evaluation predictions freeze inputs, references, adjudicator decisions and disagreements,
model/solver/parser pins, budgets, threshold grid, primary comparisons, precision/harm
limits and sampling probabilities where needed. Source corroboration is not independent
adjudication or issuer intent. Use paired family-level uncertainty, not independent
item-level intervals. Family false-acceptance and selective false-equivalence among
predicted equivalent outputs with usable reference are distinct estimands. Zero accepted
cases gives undefined selective risk. For a single prespecified one-sided 95% binomial
bound of 1% with zero observed errors, at least 299 independent accepted families are
needed; simultaneous threshold selection can require more. Conformal claims require
exchangeability and explicit finite-sample resolution. Report infeasible precision
and retain the gate, rather than substituting item or synthetic fixture counts.

## Engineering and rights

Stratify synthetic/mutation controls, source-asserted development references and
independently adjudicated natural-source evaluation. PRISMA subclauses and named
OBI/COB/STATO axioms are development evidence, not costing or issuer gold labels.
Isolate ontology release contexts. Legacy single-arm CONSORT counts cannot supply a
complete randomized partition; use typed synthetic consumer inputs before real flow
admission. Candidate native pins: typst-syntax 0.15.1; tree-sitter 0.25.10 with an
explicitly licensed exact LaTeX grammar; rustfst 1.3.1. Verify source, license, MSRV,
locked transitive graph, inventory and offline build before admission. Compare exact
untouched bytes, parser errors, unsupported macros/catcodes and weighted paths with
independent enumeration, including competing paths, epsilon transitions and rejection.
Regex is not a native CST. E21 requires authorized private restricted reference bytes,
extraction and source digests, coverage, exact staged package and separate approval.
No corpus or permission invented; no held labels, sources or data uploaded.
Engineering passing tests do not establish independent performance or rights.
Preserve all failed and inconclusive attempts.

All observed claims require actual frozen execution and review. No independent
calibration/evaluation inputs or authorized restricted corpus are presently eligible.
