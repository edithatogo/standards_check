survey projections, review receipts, versioned representation draft and

does not report a disagreement. The shared helper rejects unbound held group IDs.

conflicting source identity; no acquisition or rights gate is relaxed.

#111 open for live/replay evidence and completeness; no automatic admission,
external approval, publication claim, or source repin.
