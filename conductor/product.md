# Product Strategy

## Core Product Value
The objective is to produce a definitive, machine-readable pipeline for scholarly publication checklists (e.g., CONSORT, PRISMA). Sourcing the original published checklists and converting them into canonical representations (Markdown) that can then be processed into various outputs (PDF, LaTeX, Typst) to aid the integration of AI in scholarly publishing.

## Target Audience
Researchers, authors, reviewers, publishers, and machine-learning models assisting in the peer-review or authoring processes for academic publications.

## Primary Workflows
- **Data Quality Initiative:** Fixing links, enforcing schema validations.
- **Tooling & Automation:** Developing scripts to discover latest checklist versions and automatically generate documentation (HTML).
- **Content Expansion:** Ingesting and processing new checklists from platforms like EQUATOR.
- **Conversion Pipeline:** Transforming checklists between Markdown, Typst, LaTeX, and other formats preserving semantics and structure.
